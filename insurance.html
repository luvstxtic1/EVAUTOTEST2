Know your rights

What Arizona law actually says about your windshield claim.

Insurers sometimes make it sound complicated. It isn't. Here's the plain-language version.

$0 deductible on most comprehensive policies

Most Arizona auto insurance comprehensive coverage either waives the deductible entirely for glass repair, or applies a much smaller "glass deductible" than your standard comprehensive deductible. We check your specific policy before any work begins, so you know your exact out-of-pocket cost — often $0 — up front.

A glass claim alone can't raise your rate — ARS 20-263

Arizona statute ARS 20-263 restricts insurers from using a standalone comprehensive glass claim as grounds to raise your premium. Comprehensive claims in general are treated differently from at-fault collision claims specifically because of this kind of protection.

You choose your own shop — ARS 20-469

Under ARS 20-469, Arizona law protects a policyholder's right to select the repair shop of their choice for auto glass work. If your insurer's call center suggests a "preferred" or "in-network" shop, that's a suggestion, not a requirement — you're free to choose EV AutoGlass, and we'll work directly with your insurer on billing.
