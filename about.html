Who we are

Built to come to you, not the other way around.

EV AutoGlass started with a simple frustration: getting a windshield fixed shouldn't mean losing half a day at a shop. So we built the shop around the customer instead.

Our story

We're a mobile-first auto glass company serving the Phoenix Valley — Phoenix, Tempe, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler, Maricopa, Anthem, and everywhere in between. Every technician on our team is trained to the same standard: arrive on time, explain exactly what's happening with your vehicle, and leave it better than they found it.

We built our own insurance-handling process specifically because Arizona drivers kept telling us the same thing — they didn't know they had a choice. Now, checking your coverage and confirming your rights is part of every single quote.
