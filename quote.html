Not sure what's wrong?

That's fine — most people aren't. Here's the quick way to tell.

Small chip, under a quarter

Usually repairable in 20–30 minutes — pick "Front Windshield" and mention it's a chip.

Crack longer than a dollar bill

Typically needs a full replacement, especially if it reaches the edge of the glass.

Damage in the driver's direct line of sight

We'll almost always recommend replacement here, regardless of size, for visibility and safety.
